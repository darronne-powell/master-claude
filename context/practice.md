# The practice

> Set at /install on 2026-11-13.

## What it is

A solo AI video production practice. Darronne turns ideas, scripts and product photos into ready-to-post videos (ads, UGC-style content, explainers, avatar videos, short-form clips) in 2–3 days, with no crew, filming or studio. Sold through Upwork only; every payment goes through Upwork (fixed-price or hourly, never off-platform).

## Who it serves

From the Upwork profile, in order of fit:
- Ecommerce and Shopify brands needing product and UGC-style ads for Meta and TikTok
- Coaches, course creators and consultants needing explainers, avatar intros and short-form content
- Agencies needing a reliable white-label video partner
- Startups and small businesses with no in-house video team
- Social media managers needing a steady monthly supply

Best-fit client for the current goals: a brand or agency with a real ad budget and ongoing need, i.e. a monthly package prospect. Watch for: clients with a good freelancer rating and real spend history on Upwork.

Avoid: budgets under $25 per finished video, clients asking for free samples, off-platform contact or payment, low client ratings.

## How work comes in

**Upwork only** for now: proposals to job posts and invitations. 2 contracts so far (Oct–Nov 2026), both fixed-price, closed, paid, with feedback. No other channel yet.

## What makes you different

- Speed: first drafts in 48–72 hours.
- Full stack in one person: concept, script, voice-over, generation, edit. No hand-offs.
- Tool-agnostic: picks Veo, Runway, Sora, HeyGen or Higgsfield per project.
- Every order includes concept direction, 3 aspect ratios, captions and music, and 2 revisions.
- Identity-verified on Upwork; two completed jobs with feedback.
