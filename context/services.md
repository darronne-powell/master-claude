**Package prices assume 15–30 s videos. Final price depends on video length:** videos of 30–60 s count as 1.5 videos each, and a longer piece is quoted at the single-video price from the table above. Say this in every package proposal.

Set up on Upwork as a fixed-price contract with one milestone per month (or per batch of 4). Capacity check: Darronne works solo, so never run more than ~40 short videos a month across all retainers combined without reviewing `context/you.md` hours.

## Rate floor

- **Hourly: $10/hr minimum** (set by Darronne for the starting phase). Upwork profile rate is currently $15/hr. Hourly AI-video jobs on Upwork posted 10–50 $/hr in Nov 2026, so $15–25 is a safe asking range; $10 is the walk-away line, not the opening number.
- **Fixed-price: never below $25 per finished video**, whatever the job post's budget. Posts at $5–12 for a full video are skipped or answered with a scoped-down offer.

## Scope rules

- **Included:** short concept and style direction before starting, 3 aspect ratios (9:16, 1:1, 16:9), captions, music, sound effects, HD files, 2 revision rounds.
- **Extra:** additional revision round $15; rush delivery under 24 h +30%; extra length billed at the next tier; raw project files or AI source clips +$20; paid stock or premium voice licences passed through at cost.
- **Not offered:** live filming, real-actor UGC, anything that impersonates a real person without written consent.

## Payment terms

- **The one rule: every payment happens on Upwork.** Any Upwork contract type is fine: fixed-price (one payment or several milestones) or hourly. What's never allowed is payment, or a request for payment, outside Upwork (Upwork's terms forbid it, and it removes payment protection). Never suggest it to a client, and decline politely if a client suggests it. (Clarified by Darronne 2026-11-13: "Upwork milestones" means "Upwork only", not a specific contract type.)
- Fixed-price is the default for one-off videos. Splitting larger jobs (over $150) into 2 milestones (first draft / final delivery) is a suggestion, not a requirement.
