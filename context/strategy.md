# Strategy

> Set at /install on 2026-11-13. Kept honest by /log's drift check and /weekly-review.

## Priorities right now (in order)

1. **Five-star reviews, fast.** The account is new (2 jobs, 2 reviews). Every job, one-off or not, is delivered to earn 5 stars: over-communicate, deliver early, stay inside the two revision rounds without friction. A bad review costs more than a lost job right now, so skip any job that looks likely to end badly (vague scope, unrealistic budget, poor client rating).
2. **Land the first monthly package client.** This is the main goal. Recurring work is what makes the income target reachable for one person. Every one-off client who's happy gets offered the Starter package after delivery.
3. **Then raise prices**, following the raise plan in `context/services.md`.

One-off jobs are welcome along the way; they feed priority 1.

## What success looks like (3–6 months)

- **$2,000+/month minimum, aiming for $3,000+/month**, all through Upwork.
- At least 1 and ideally 3 monthly package clients.
- 10+ jobs with five-star feedback; prices moved up at least one step on the raise plan.

The arithmetic: $2,000/month at starter prices is about 40 short videos, or 3 Starter packages ($1,080) plus ~18 one-off videos. $3,000 needs packages and the first price raise, which is why priorities 2 and 3 matter.

## How Claude helps (Darronne's ask, 2026-11-13)

Claude runs the pipeline end to end, Darronne approves and delivers:

1. **Find jobs:** search Upwork daily for jobs that fit `services.md`, skip red flags, rank the best.
2. **Write proposals:** `/proposal` for each, priced from `services.md`, with portfolio pieces Darronne picks.
3. **Write scripts** for won jobs.
4. **Follow up** with leads and clients, and offer the monthly package after every good delivery.

**Approval line:** Claude drafts everything; **Darronne gives an explicit yes before anything is submitted or sent** (each proposal costs Connects, each message goes out under Darronne's name). That never changes, however routine the task gets.
