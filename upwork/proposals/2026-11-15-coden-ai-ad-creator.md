AI ADS

Product accuracy is where AI ads lose agency clients. Kling and Veo redraw the label on every generation, so the bottle that looks right in shot 1 has different text and a slightly different cap by shot 4.

For a seven-figure DTC brand, that's an ad the client rejects, or worse, one that runs with the wrong packaging.

How I keep products accurate:
1. Build start and end frames from the brand's real product photos (Nano Banana for the stills), so the video tool animates the true packaging.
2. Keep hero product shots short and controlled, and let the people and the story carry the longer shots.
3. When a label still drifts, I composite the real label back in Premiere Pro or After Effects.
