## Letter

Anti-vaping ads on Meta and TikTok can get caught by the same automated review that blocks vape sales ads, especially when a device or a cloud of vapor is on screen in the first frames.

If that happens, the campaign sits in review or gets rejected, and the budget set aside for it has nowhere to run.

So the metaphor has two jobs. It has to land in the first 2 seconds with the sound off, and it has to carry the message without leaning on close-ups of the device.

A free idea, 3 lines:
Open on a dog leash clipped to a teenager's belt loop, with nobody holding the other end.
Every time she reaches for something (a friend, a game, sleep), the leash snaps tight.
End card: "Nicotine holds the other end."

How I'd approach yours:
1. Send 2 or 3 metaphor concepts as short written treatments with one reference frame each, so you pick a direction before anything is generated.
2. Script and storyboard the chosen concept shot by shot, with captions written for sound-off viewing.
3. Produce it with AI video (Veo, Kling, Runway), then edit, add sound design and music in Premiere Pro and After Effects.
4. Deliver 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 cuts, plus a 6 second version for hook testing.
