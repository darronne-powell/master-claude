## Letter

Bug spray is one of the harder products for AI video: ask Kling or Veo for someone spraying their arms and you often get a mist that looks like smoke, a can with a warped label, and nobody reacting to a single bug.

That matters because the first 3 seconds of a bug spray ad are the bite, the swat or the itch. If that moment looks fake, people scroll before the can shows up.

You wrote that you'll share "my entire workflow and all the necessary information." That tells me you already have a format that sells, and what you need is someone who follows it and keeps the ads coming at a steady pace.

How I'd approach yours:
1. Go through your workflow and past ads first, and match your hooks, pacing and caption style.
2. Build the product shots from your real product photos as start frames, so the label and colors stay accurate in every clip.
3. Keep the spray moment short, and spend the screen time on the person and the result: a quiet evening outside with no bites.
4. Deliver 9:16, 1:1 and 16:9 with captions and music.

A free idea for your first ad, 3 lines:
Hook: a slap on the arm, then "Third one tonight."
Middle: two sprays, she sits back down, the buzzing stops.
End: "Finally get to finish my coffee outside."
